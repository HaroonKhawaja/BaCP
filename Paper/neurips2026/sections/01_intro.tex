\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Unstructured pruning removes weights from a trained network and retrains what
remains \citep{han2015learning}. Up to 90--95\% sparsity this is close to free:
a cubic gradual schedule \citep{zhu2018prune,gale2019state} recovers essentially
dense accuracy on CIFAR-scale classifiers. Past that point retraining stops
being repair and becomes re-derivation --- the network must rebuild a
representation from a support too small to hold the one it had --- and methods
within a fraction of a point at 90\% separate by tens of points
\citep{li2025east}.

Cross-entropy supplies at most $\log_2 C$ bits per example and constrains the
decisions, not the representation they are read from --- which is exactly what
the mask destroyed. A contrastive term against a frozen dense teacher instead
supplies a $D$-dimensional target direction per example; the appendix develops
the mechanism.

That objective is not new. \citet{xu2022cap} introduced Contrastive Pruning
(CAP): cross-entropy plus three contrastive terms against frozen pretrained
(PrC), task-fine-tuned (FiC) and snapshot (SnC) teachers, on BERT-family
encoders to 97\% sparsity. \textbf{The PrC/FiC/SnC decomposition, and the
four-term objective that combines them with cross-entropy, are CAP's, not
ours.} We state it here because it bounds what follows;
Section~\ref{sec:method} sets out where our instantiation differs.

We contribute a controlled study of that objective --- \emph{Backbone
Contrastive Pruning} (BaCP) --- in three settings CAP did not examine:
(i) convolutional vision backbones, ResNet-34/50 and VGG-11/19;
(ii) criterion-agnosticism --- magnitude \citep{han2015learning}, SNIP-it
\citep{lee2019snip,verdenius2020snipit} and WANDA \citep{sun2024wanda} under one
global threshold, the criterion the only variable between blocks; and
(iii) sparsity to 99.9\%, where CAP reported 97\%. Every BaCP run was matched
against an iterative pruning (I.P.) baseline matched in schedule, criterion,
epoch budget and fine-tuning stage, so $\Delta$, not any absolute accuracy, is
the unit of evidence.

\paragraph{Related work.} The prune--retrain template and its cubic
ramp-then-hold schedule \citep{han2015learning,zhu2018prune,gale2019state} are
adopted here. At-init and lottery-ticket
criteria \citep{frankle2019lottery,wang2020grasp,tanaka2020synflow} meet layer
collapse in the extreme regime; dynamic sparse training
\citep{mocanu2018set,evci2020rigl,liu2021granet,li2025east} is context, not a
baseline: the mask here is static, and objective- and support-level
interventions are separable. The loss forms are SimCLR's and SupCon's
\citep{chen2020simclr,khosla2020supcon}, with CRD \citep{tian2020crd} the
antecedent for a contrastive term where logit matching
\citep{hinton2015distilling} would go. \citet{blalock2020state} document how
rarely pruning papers are comparable, the motivation for the matched-budget
design; the appendix treats each line in full.
