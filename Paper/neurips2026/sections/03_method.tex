\section{Method and protocol}
\label{sec:method}
\label{sec:experiments}

\paragraph{Pipeline.}
Four stages per cell: ImageNet-pretrained initialisation
\citep{deng2009imagenet}; a dense fine-tune giving the reference model; gradual
pruning under one of two objectives; a fine-tune with the mask frozen. That is
\citet{han2015learning}'s prune--retrain on
\citet{zhu2018prune}'s schedule, with \citet{xu2022cap}'s contrastive
supervision at stage three --- the only place the arms differ.

\paragraph{Objective.}
With $z = \mathrm{normalize}(g(f_\theta(x)))$ --- so $z_i^{\top} z_j$ is cosine
similarity --- $\hat z$ a frozen teacher's and $\tau = 0.15$, an anchor $i$ with
positives $P(i)$ among $N$ candidates gave
%
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:functional}
\mathcal{L}_i \;=\; -\frac{1}{|P(i)|}\sum_{j \in P(i)}
\log \frac{\exp\!\left(z_i^{\top}\hat z_j / \tau\right)}
          {\sum_{k=1}^{N}\exp\!\left(z_i^{\top}\hat z_k / \tau\right)} ,
\end{equation}
%
Equation~1 of \citet{xu2022cap}. BaCP trained under
$\mathcal{L} = \lambda_1 \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{PrC}} + \lambda_2
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{FiC}} + \lambda_3 \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{SnC}} + \lambda_4
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CE}}$, every $\lambda_i = 0.25$, untuned and
simplex-constrained so rebalancing cannot also change the effective learning
rate. Teachers ran under \texttt{no\_grad}: each term was a distillation, not a
joint embedding, objective. Each module summed a self-supervised instantiation,
$P(i) = \{\hat z_i\}$, and a supervised one, $P(i) = \{\hat z_j : y_j = y_i\}$
\citep{khosla2020supcon}, over one denominator and three teachers: pretrained,
dense fine-tuned, and the run's own snapshots. Over $K$ snapshots
SnC was \emph{averaged}, $\frac{1}{K}\sum_k$, not summed, so its scale and
$\lambda_3$ stay independent of $K$.

\paragraph{Pruning.}
Sparsity followed \citet{zhu2018prune}'s cubic schedule,
%
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:cubic}
s_t \;=\; s_f\left(1 - \left(1 - \frac{t}{T}\right)^{\!3}\right),
\end{equation}
%
ramped over the first 80\% of steps, frozen at $s_f$ for the last 20\%
\citep{gale2019state}, recomputed every $\Delta T = 100$ steps. Scores were
$|w_{ij}|$ \citep{han2015learning},
$|w_{ij}\,\partial\mathcal{L}/\partial w_{ij}|$ (SNIP's score,
\citealp{lee2019snip}; iterated after \citealp{verdenius2020snipit}) and
$|w_{ij}|\lVert X_j\rVert_2$
\citep{sun2024wanda}, all thresholded at one \emph{global} value, so
Table~\ref{tab:main}'s blocks differ only in the score. The global grouping
is ours: WANDA's authors report row-wise scoring does not transfer to image
classifiers. The classifier head was pruned, following vision practice
\citep{evci2020rigl,liu2021granet}; the projection head never was, and was
discarded before the fine-tune. Prunable scope was identical across arms.

\paragraph{Setup.}
Backbones were ResNet-34/50 \citep{he2016deep} and VGG-11/19
\citep{simonyan2015very}: ResNet-50 carries 53 BatchNorm layers, the VGGs none
and $\approx$85\% of their parameters in the classifier MLP. CIFAR-10
\citep{krizhevsky2009cifar} split \num{45000}/\num{5000}/\num{10000}
train/validation/held-out test; batch 512 gave 87 steps per epoch, and every
accuracy covers all \num{10000} test images. The grid ran 100 cells: 4 models
$\times$ 3 criteria $\times$ 4 sparsities $\times$ 2 arms, plus 4 dense.
BaCP's contrastive phase selected on \emph{training loss}, lacking a
supervised validation signal, both fine-tunes on \emph{validation accuracy};
the test set selected nothing. Headline cells ran
3 seeds, the rest single-seed, against a measured floor of
\result{cifar10.noise.floor.lo}--%
\result{cifar10.noise.floor.hi} points \citep{bouthillier2021variance}.

\paragraph{Matched budget.}
The I.P.\ baseline is not a separately tuned competitor but the \emph{same run}
--- same pretrained initialisation, same dense checkpoint, same criterion, same
cubic schedule, same $\Delta T$, same prunable scope, same 50 pruning epochs,
same 25-epoch AdamW $10^{-4}$ fine-tune \citep{loshchilov2019adamw}, same splits,
same bf16 autocast, same gradient clipping at global norm 10.0 (SNIP-it's
published value \citep{verdenius2020snipit}, applied symmetrically because an
asymmetric stabiliser would itself be a difference between arms) --- with
Eq.~\eqref{eq:functional} replaced by plain cross-entropy. The fine-tune ran on
both arms: without it the comparison degenerates into 75 epochs of BaCP against
50 of I.P.\ and the objective cannot be separated from the extra training.
\citet{blalock2020state} document how often pruning comparisons fail here;
Table~\ref{tab:protocol} gives the protocol.

\paragraph{The one asymmetry.}
\label{sec:experiments:vgglr}
BaCP's pruning phase used SGD 0.1 on the ResNets, 0.05 on the VGGs; I.P.\ stayed
at 0.01, the objectives not sharing a gradient scale. On
\texttt{vgg19}/\texttt{magnitude}/0.95, everything else identical, LR 0.10
reached \result{vgg19.probelr010.magnitude.0.95}, LR 0.05
\result{vgg19.probelr005.magnitude.0.95} and matched I.P.\
\result{vgg19.probeip.magnitude.0.95}. Fixed before the sweep from this single
cell and applied uniformly to both VGGs; I.P.'s rate was not tuned in reply,
conservative in the direction that matters.

The uniform choice was confirmed, not merely assumed, once the sweep completed:
\texttt{vgg11}/\texttt{magnitude} was re-run at LR 0.1 across all four
sparsities. 0.05 wins at 0.95
(\result{vgg11.bacp.magnitude.0.95} vs \result{vgg11.probelr010.magnitude.0.95}),
0.97 (\result{vgg11.bacp.magnitude.0.97} vs
\result{vgg11.probelr010.magnitude.0.97}) and 0.99
(\result{vgg11.bacp.magnitude.0.99} vs \result{vgg11.probelr010.magnitude.0.99});
0.1 is marginally ahead only at 0.999
(\result{vgg11.probelr010.magnitude.0.999} vs \result{vgg11.bacp.magnitude.0.999}),
where both arms sit closest to the I.P.\ baseline and the gap is smallest. The
architecture argument -- zero BatchNorm layers, unable to absorb the higher
step size -- was the reason for trying 0.05 in the first place; this is the
measurement that shows it was the right call on both models, not only the one
it was measured on.
