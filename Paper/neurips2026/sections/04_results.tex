\paragraph{Across sparsity.}
The margin widened at every step of the schedule. Mean $\Delta$ rose
\result{summary.delta.sparsity.0.95}, \result{summary.delta.sparsity.0.97},
\result{summary.delta.sparsity.0.99} and
\result{summary.delta.sparsity.0.999} at 0.95, 0.97, 0.99 and 0.999 --
strictly monotone, over twelve cells per column spanning four architectures
and three criteria. At 0.95 both arms sit near the dense reference and there
is little damage to repair. By 0.999 the baseline has lost the network:
ResNet-50 under WANDA falls to \result{resnet50.ip.wanda.0.999}, barely above
chance on ten classes, and the contrastive objective returns
\result{resnet50.delta.wanda.0.999}. That monotonicity is the paper's central
result, and it is the shape a representation-level effect predicts. The mean
over the whole grid, \result{summary.delta.mean}, is the wrong summary of it:
that figure is dominated by the 0.999 column and conceals the trend that makes
the mechanism legible.

\paragraph{Across criteria.}
No block reverses in aggregate: mean $\Delta$ is
\result{summary.delta.criterion.magnitude} under magnitude,
\result{summary.delta.criterion.snip} under SNIP-it and
\result{summary.delta.criterion.wanda} under WANDA. The three share everything
but the score, so this is the criterion-agnosticism evidence. The ordering is
not an independent finding: WANDA leads because it damages the model most and
therefore leaves the most to recover, which is the sparsity trend read along a
second axis. Sign consistency is not universal --
\result{summary.delta.npositive} of \result{summary.delta.ncells} pairs are
positive -- and the negatives are not scattered but concentrated in a single
model, which the next paragraph takes up rather than averaging away.

\paragraph{Where it does not help.}
VGG-11 is negative on average, \result{summary.delta.model.vgg11}, against
\result{summary.delta.model.resnet50} for ResNet-50,
\result{summary.delta.model.resnet34} for ResNet-34 and
\result{summary.delta.model.vgg19} for VGG-19. Its own baseline explains why:
I.P. at 0.95 reaches \result{vgg11.ip.magnitude.0.95} against a dense
\result{vgg11.dense.none.0.0}, so gradual pruning costs it almost nothing even
at 95\% sparsity, and there is no representation damage to repair. Roughly
85\% of VGG-11's parameters sit in a classifier MLP that is largely redundant
for $32\times32$ inputs. A contrastive term added to an objective already at
its ceiling is a competing gradient, and it costs a few tenths. We report this
as the boundary condition of the claim rather than as an anomaly: the
mechanism that yields \result{summary.delta.sparsity.0.999} where pruning
destroys the network predicts approximately nothing where pruning does no
harm, and that is what we measured.

\paragraph{Beyond the grid.}
Two checks outside Table~\ref{tab:main} test whether the effect is tied to the
dense-convolutional shapes above or to CIFAR-10. MobileNetV2 --- inverted
residual, entirely depthwise-separable, \num{2.2}M prunable weights against
ResNet-34's \num{21}M --- reproduces the pattern across all three criteria, and
so does ResNet-34 on CIFAR-100, where BaCP leads all twelve cells. Both run at
three seeds under the same matched protocol, and both show the ordering
Table~\ref{tab:main} shows: small margins while the network is intact, large
ones where the baseline begins to fail. Appendix~\ref{app:beyond} gives the
tables and the two qualifications that belong with them --- WANDA's statistic
is undefined for grouped convolutions, and MobileNetV2 is dead at 0.999 for
both arms at every learning rate we tried.

\paragraph{Which contrastive objective.}
Table~\ref{tab:ablation} changes two keys: the contrastive formulation and the
projection-head mode (\S\ref{sec:cap}). \emph{Legacy} is the stacked
$2B \times 2B$ form with per-model trainable heads; \emph{control} is CAP's
\citep{xu2022cap} rectangular $B \times N$ functional with one frozen shared
head. Legacy led control at every measured sparsity --- why it is the objective
studied, and why the losing arm is reported --- and both contrastive arms sat at
or above I.P.: the direction does not depend on the realisation, only its size.
The legacy margin held near
\result{resnet50.abldelta.magnitude.0.95} through
\result{resnet50.abldelta.magnitude.0.97} points before rising to
\result{resnet50.abldelta.magnitude.0.99} at 0.99 --- so the grid was extended
to 0.999. Caveats: single seed; a gap above but not far above the
\result{cifar10.noise.floor.lo}--\result{cifar10.noise.floor.hi}-point noise
floor; an earlier protocol, so ordering only; and a winner carrying the
trainable head (Section~\ref{sec:discussion}). That ordering is not what our
account of the mechanism predicts, and we have none for it.
