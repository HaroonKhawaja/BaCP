\paragraph{Across sparsity.}
0.95 is the least informative comparison: both arms sit near the dense
reference, with little damage for either objective to repair. The
informative region is the right end of the schedule, where the weight budget is
small enough that the retraining signal has to do real work, and a
representation-level effect predicts the margin widening there.
% TODO: confirm direction once the sweep lands -- the claim "the margin widened with sparsity" must be checked against every model column, not only ResNet-50.

\paragraph{Across criteria.}
The three blocks share everything but the score, so a $\Delta$ that keeps its
sign across all three is the criterion-agnosticism evidence; consistency of sign
matters, not their ranking. If any block reverses sign, the claim
is narrowed to the criteria where it holds and the reversal reported rather than
averaged away.
% TODO: confirm direction once the sweep lands -- if any criterion block reverses sign, the criterion-agnostic claim must be narrowed to the criteria where it holds, and the reversal reported rather than averaged away.

\paragraph{Across architectures and seeds.}
ResNet and VGG are not expected to behave identically --- heavily normalised and
convolution-dominated against unnormalised and classifier-MLP-dominated --- and
the learning-rate probe of Section~\ref{sec:method} is one place the difference
already surfaced. The daggered cells carry three seeds, and a $\Delta$ that does
not clear its own seed spread is not a result.

\paragraph{Which contrastive objective.}
Table~\ref{tab:ablation} changes exactly two configuration keys: the contrastive
formulation and the projection-head mode. \emph{Legacy} is SupCon plus NT-Xent
over the square $2B \times 2B$ matrix with per-model trainable heads;
\emph{control} is CAP's \citep{xu2022cap} rectangular $B \times N$ functional
with one frozen head
shared by student and teachers, so the head cannot absorb the objective. Legacy
was ahead of control at every measured sparsity, which is why it is the
objective studied and why we report the arm that lost. Both contrastive arms
were at or above I.P.\ everywhere measured, so the direction does not depend on
the realisation, only its size. The legacy margin grew as sparsity rose ---
\result{resnet50.abldelta.magnitude.0.95},
\result{resnet50.abldelta.magnitude.0.97},
\result{resnet50.abldelta.magnitude.0.99} points --- which is why the grid was
extended to 0.999. Caveats: single seed; a gap exceeding but not far exceeding
the \result{cifar10.noise.floor.lo}--\result{cifar10.noise.floor.hi}-point noise
floor; an earlier protocol, so ordering only; and a winner carrying the
trainable head (Section~\ref{sec:discussion}). That ordering is not what our
account of the mechanism predicts, and we have no mechanism for it.

\paragraph{Calibration.}
Gradual magnitude pruning is our magnitude criterion renamed:
ResNet-50 / CIFAR-10 / 95\% GMP reached \resultpm{lit.granet.resnet50.gmp.0.95}
\citep{liu2021granet} under 160 epochs at batch 128, against our 50$+$25 at
batch 512. That row carries one load --- whether the baseline BaCP is measured
against is competently trained --- not a ranking. Two gaps we state rather than paper
over: VGG-11 has no published comparator at any sparsity, and no published
static comparator exists at 0.97, 0.99 or 0.999 for any of our four models,
which is exactly why the I.P.\ baseline was run under a matched budget rather
than quoted. The appendix (Table~\ref{tab:published}) collects the published
values.
