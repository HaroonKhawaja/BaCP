\section{Relation to CAP}
\label{sec:cap}

CAP \citep{xu2022cap} sums cross-entropy with three contrastive terms during
gradual pruning, one per frozen teacher --- pretrained (PrC), task-fine-tuned
(FiC), snapshot (SnC) --- on language encoders.

That four-term decomposition is CAP's, not ours; ours permutes only the
$\lambda$ indices and is not presented as new. Nothing below claims superiority.

CAP computes one functional over the rectangular $B \times N$
student-anchor/teacher-candidate matrix, contrasting \texttt{[CLS]} with no
projection head; we stacked both embeddings and summed SupCon and NT-Xent over
the square $2B \times 2B$ matrix on $\mathrm{cat}[z_s, z_t]$, SimCLR all-pairs,
with in-batch negatives and a trainable, never-pruned head; CAP's memory bank we
omitted rather than improved on --- it slots into that form unchanged, leaving
only the batch.

The loss form is a real difference, not a reparameterisation; both were run and
measurement decided (\S\ref{sec:results}, Table~\ref{tab:ablation}). The head is
the substantive caveat (\S\ref{sec:discussion}). Table~\ref{tab:cap-diff} lists
the rest. Ours is CAP's objective on vision backbones, three pruning criteria,
sparsities beyond CAP's.
